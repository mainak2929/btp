## Check 2: gold vs international gold × USD/INR

The international price is a monthly *average* (and USD/INR a monthly average of daily rates), so month by month it lags month-end prices; the cumulative change is the fair comparison.

| Month | International gold in ₹ (avg) | Change since Dec 2025 | Our gold series, change since Dec 2025 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Jan 2026 | ₹432,060/oz | +11.4% | +23.5% |

| Mar 2026 | ₹450,725/oz | +16.2% | +10.1% |

| Sep 2026 | ₹412,518/oz | +6.3% | +10.5% |
| Oct 2026 | ₹392,096/oz | +1.1% | +4.7% |

Both move the same way; after ten months our gold series is a few percent above the international price in rupees. Month-end vs monthly-average timing explains part of it (gold swung ±5–10% a month in 2026); the rest is the Indian price's premium or discount to the world price, which also moved in earlier years (see verification.md).
